### Finance KPI: Revenue and Forecast Dashboard

#### Key Business Questions Answered

The model was designed to support finance and commercial stakeholders in answering the following business questions:

* How is revenue performing compared to last month?
* How is revenue performing compared to the same period last year?
* How much revenue is recurring and subscription-based?
* What revenue has already been realised versus what is forecasted?
* Are we on track to achieve annual targets?
* What is the projected year-end revenue position?
* How do performance outcomes differ under base, committed, target, and stretch forecast scenarios?
* What are the long-term revenue trends when seasonality is removed?

One of the key challenges in this project was creating a realistic view of annual revenue performance while the financial year was still in progress.
The business required a KPI framework that could simultaneously report:

* Revenue already received
* Expected recurring subscription revenue
* Sales pipeline forecasts
* Full-year projected performance

To achieve this, I developed a hybrid Actual + Forecast model. More is discussed in the Finance KPI folder.
